Common lawn problems

Where most irrigation systems quietly fail your lawn.

Most homeowners don't realize their irrigation system is working against them. Poor coverage, over-watering, broken heads, and outdated timers all add up to a stressed lawn and a high water bill.

Uneven coverage

Dry patches and over-saturated zones hurt your lawn's health.

When sprinkler heads are misaligned, spaced incorrectly, or clogged, some areas flood while others wither.

  • · Dead patches appear despite regular watering
  • · Overlapping zones waste water and inflate your bill
Outdated timers

Fixed schedules ignore rain, soil type, and seasonal change.

Old mechanical timers water the same amount in a rainstorm as they do during a heat wave.

  • · Watering during rain wastes thousands of gallons
  • · Schedules don't adjust for temperature changes
Winter damage

Skipping winterization cracks pipes and ruins heads.

Water left in irrigation lines during the first freeze expands and cracks PVC pipes and valve manifolds.

  • · Cracked pipes go unnoticed until spring start-up
  • · Backflow preventers are the most expensive to replace
Neglected maintenance

Small problems compound into expensive system failures.

A single broken head, a slow valve leak, or a misaligned rotor seems minor — until the water bill spikes.

  • · Broken heads spray sidewalks and driveways
  • · Annual inspections catch issues before they compound

Poor coverage → Outdated timers → Freeze damage → Neglect → System failure

How much does a sprinkler system installation cost?

Installation costs vary based on property size, number of zones, and system complexity. Most residential installs range from $2,500 to $6,000. We provide free on-site evaluations with transparent, no-surprise quotes before any work begins.

Will installation damage my lawn or landscaping?

We use directional boring equipment that minimizes surface disruption. Small entry and exit points are made where necessary, and our crew carefully replaces any turf disturbed. Most lawns show no visible difference within a week.

How long does installation take?

Most residential installations are completed in a single day. Larger properties may take two days. After installation, we walk you through the full system and program your smart controller before we leave.

Do I need to winterize my system every year?

Yes — in climates where temperatures drop below freezing, annual winterization (blow-out) is essential. Water left in pipes and heads can freeze and crack, leading to costly repairs. We offer seasonal plans that include both spring start-up and fall blow-out.

Will a sprinkler system save me money on my water bill?

In most cases, yes. A properly designed irrigation system waters more efficiently than hand watering — delivering the right amount to each zone at the optimal time. Many homeowners see 20–40% reductions in outdoor water usage after installation.

Can you service a system installed by someone else?

Absolutely. We service and repair all makes and models of irrigation systems regardless of who installed them. We'll diagnose the issue, explain what's needed, and provide a clear quote before doing any repair work.
